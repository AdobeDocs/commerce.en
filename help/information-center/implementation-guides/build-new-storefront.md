# Build a new storefront


Adobe Commerce customers using any version of the platform can build a new storefront using Edge Delivery Services, Commerce drop-in components, and content blocks.

Starting from the Adobe Commerce boilerplate gives you a working storefront that you can customize and connect to your own Adobe Commerce or [!DNL Adobe Commerce Optimizer] instance.

>[!NOTE]
>
>This guide applies to Adobe Commerce (on Cloud or on premises), Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service, Adobe Commerce Optimizer connected to Adobe Commerce, and stand-alone Adobe Commerce Optimizer with an existing or third-party backend. See [Architecture options at a glance](../architecture-options/overview.md#architecture-options-at-a-glance) to confirm which option applies to your project.

## Where to start

1. Review the [Adobe Commerce Storefront overview](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/developer/commerce/storefront/) to understand the architecture and available drop-in components.

1. Follow the [storefront developer guide](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/developer/commerce/storefront/get-started/) to scope, set up, and launch a project.

1. Connect the storefront to your Commerce backend.

   Follow the instructions based on your deployment model. For details, see [Backend options](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/tools/commerce-storefront/get-started/backends/){target="_blank"} in the _Storefront Developer Guide_.
