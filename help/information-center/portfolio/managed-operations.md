# [!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] with Managed Operations

**Launch faster and run with confidence.**

[!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] is a fully managed, versionless SaaS platform. Adobe hosts, scales, secures, and updates the core application and infrastructure, so your team can focus on building great shopping experiences.

**[!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] with Managed Operations** is a premium offering that pairs the platform with a dedicated Adobe Managed Services team and a named Customer Success Engineer (CSE). Together, they monitor, guide, and optimize your complete commerce solution, including your [!DNL App Builder] applications, integrations, and [!DNL Commerce Storefront] powered by [!DNL Edge Delivery Services], from onboarding through launch and ongoing operations.

## Customer Success Engineer

Your named Customer Success Engineer (CSE) is your single point of contact at Adobe and an expert in the full [!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] toolset, including [!DNL App Builder], [!DNL API Mesh], and [!DNL Commerce Storefront] powered by [!DNL Edge Delivery Services]. Your CSE works with you and your implementation partners to:

- Guide and support you through the onboarding process
- Manage provisioning and platform setup
- Advise on architectural principles for integrations and customizations, including [!DNL App Builder] applications and [!DNL Commerce Storefront] powered by [!DNL Edge Delivery Services]
- Support the [!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] update process
- Provide event support through planning, execution, and monitoring
- Coordinate your go-live with milestone checklists and a dedicated go-live war room
- Create and maintain a runbook specific to your setup
- Run regular health and optimization assessments, and present recommendations

>[!NOTE]
>
>Your CSE provides guidance and advisory services. You (or your implementation partner) remain responsible for developing and maintaining your customizations. See [Shared responsibility security and operational model](/help/cloud-service/security/shared-responsibility.md).

## Proactive monitoring and incident response

The Managed Services team monitors your complete solution around the clock (not just the platform), so issues can be addressed before they affect your shoppers:

- **Performance monitoring**—Continuous tracking of page load times, response times, and availability.
- **Traffic monitoring**—Analysis of traffic patterns to detect spikes or unusual behavior that may indicate issues, threats, or capacity needs.
- **Automated alerting**—Alerts for performance degradation, downtime, or security events, with proactive outreach from your CSE.
- **Enhanced response**—Faster time to first response and resolution, with regular updates during priority incidents and a root cause analysis for qualifying incidents.

## Security

[!DNL Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service] secures the platform, infrastructure, and core application. With Managed Operations, you also get:

- **Proactive security guidance**—Expert guidance to keep your out-of-process customizations secure and compliant.
- **Web Application Firewall (WAF) tuning and reporting**—Rules tuned to your traffic beyond the standard global ruleset, with regular reports on what was blocked.
- **Advanced rate limiting**—Additional protection against volumetric attacks.
- **Security audits and risk assessments**—Regular reviews of vulnerabilities with a plan to mitigate them.
- **Incident response**—Immediate action plans for security incidents to minimize impact and downtime.
